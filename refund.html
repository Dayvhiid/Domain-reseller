Refund Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1

Overview

At DomainPlatform, we aim to provide reliable domain registration and infrastructure services. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued for services purchased through our platform.

Due to the nature of digital domain services and third-party registry fees, not all purchases are eligible for refund. Please review this policy carefully before making a purchase.

2

Domain Registration Refunds

Domain registration fees are generally non-refundable once the registration is completed with the registry. This is because:

  • Registry fees are paid immediately upon registration and cannot be recovered.
  • Domain names are considered digital goods once provisioned and delegated.
  • ICANN and registry policies do not require registrars to offer refunds on completed registrations.

Exception: If a domain registration fails due to a technical error on our platform before reaching the registry, a full refund will be issued automatically within 5 business days.

3

Renewal & Transfer Fees

  • Renewals: Domain renewal fees are non-refundable once the renewal is processed by the registry. If you cancel an auto-renewal before it is processed, no charge will be applied.
  • Transfers In: Domain transfer fees are non-refundable once the transfer is initiated. If a transfer fails due to registry restrictions, a full refund will be issued.
  • Transfers Out: No fee is charged for transferring a domain away from DomainPlatform. No refund applies as there is no charge.
4

Hosting & Infrastructure Services

For hosting, DNS, and other infrastructure services:

  • Monthly Plans: Refund requests for monthly billing cycles must be made within 7 days of the charge. No partial-month refunds are issued.
  • Annual Plans: A prorated refund may be issued within 30 days of the initial purchase. After 30 days, no refunds are provided for the remaining balance.
  • Setup Fees: One-time setup fees are non-refundable once the service has been provisioned.
5

Eligible Refund Scenarios

You may be eligible for a full or partial refund in the following situations:

  • Domain registration failed due to a technical error on our platform.
  • Domain transfer failed due to a platform-side issue (not registry restrictions).
  • You were charged for a service that was never provisioned or delivered.
  • Duplicate charges were applied to your account for the same service.
  • A service outage exceeded our SLA guarantees, resulting in service credits or refunds.
!

Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for refunds under any circumstance:

  • Successfully registered or renewed domain names.
  • Premium domain name purchases.
  • WHOIS privacy protection services.
  • SSL certificates that have been issued and installed.
  • Redemption fees paid to recover expired domains from the registry.
  • Services canceled after the applicable refund window has passed.
7

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your DomainPlatform account.
  2. Navigate to Billing → Transaction History.
  3. Locate the transaction you wish to dispute and click Request Refund.
  4. Select a reason for your refund request and provide any supporting details.
  5. Submit the request. Our billing team will review it within 3 business days.

Alternatively, you can email billing@domainplatform.com with your account email, transaction ID, and reason for the refund request.

8

Processing Times

Once a refund is approved, processing times vary by payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: 5–10 business days.
  • PayPal: 3–5 business days.
  • Bank Transfer: 7–14 business days.
  • Account Credit: Applied instantly upon approval.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase. If that method is unavailable, we will work with you to find an alternative.

9

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Refund Policy or need assistance with a refund request, please reach out:

  • Billing Support: billing@domainplatform.com
  • General Support: support@domainplatform.com
